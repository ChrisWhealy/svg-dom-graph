svg-dom-graph

A minimal directed graph: three boxes connected by straight, arrow-tipped connectors.

Connector routing

Drag either box. The connector reroutes automatically, whichever routing style is selected below.

Switch to "Elbow", then drag the slider to round its corners.

Increase the radius, then drag the boxes close together and the slider's max maximum is pulled down to whatever radius still fits, with no error.

Drag the boxes apart again, and the slider's own ceiling dynamically rises — but its value stays where it was left, since it now represents the currently applicable radius, not some remembered value from a previous request. Move the slider again to reclaim any of the room that's returned.

Fixing points

The parent starts with one child. Their straight connector aims at each box's centre, stopping at whichever boundary point that ray crosses first.

Move the slider to 1, and the connector snaps onto the exact midpoint of the side it crosses. Move it higher, and more children appear, sharing the same parent — each connector then settles on its own evenly spaced fixing point along the parent's side. Move it back down, and children disappear again, always leaving at least one.
