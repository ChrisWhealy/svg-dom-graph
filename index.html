svg-dom-graph

A minimal directed graph: three boxes connected by straight, arrow-tipped connectors.

Connector routing

Drag either box. The connector reroutes automatically, whichever routing style is selected below.

Switch to "Elbow", then drag the slider to round its corners. Increase the radius, then drag the boxes close together — the rendered corners shrink to fit, with no error. Drag the boxes apart again, and the full requested radius returns automatically.
