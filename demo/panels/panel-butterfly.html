Radix-2 Butterfly

The butterfly operation is the building block of a fast Fourier transform. As the name implies, the Radix-2 version of the Butterfly reads the two values being combined (a and b) and a third twiddle factor w. The value of b is then multipled by the twiddle factor and the result is the sum and difference of a + w·b and a − w·b.

In this example, a is 2 + i and b is 1 + 2i. The slider sets the value of k that appears in the calculation of the twiddle factor w = e−2πik/8. This is hard-coded to be an eight-point transform, so the multiplication rotates b by an eighth of a turn for each successive step.

See the Roots of unity section in the demo panel for complex numbers.

As with all other operator nodes, the Butterfly does not perform any calculations. It simply displays the values calculated in plain Rust.
