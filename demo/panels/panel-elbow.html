Connector routing

As you drag either box, the connector reroutes automatically. The connector will reroute using the selected routing style.

Switch to "Elbow", then drag the slider to round its corners.

Increase the radius, then drag the boxes close together and the slider's max maximum is pulled down to accommodate whatever radius will fit in the available space.

Drag the boxes apart again, and the slider's own ceiling dynamically rises, but its value stays where it was left, since it now represents the currently applicable radius, not some remembered value from a previous request. Move the slider again to reclaim any of the room that repositioning the nodes have returned.
