Cell selection: hexdump

A DataFormat::HexDump node shows its u8 values as hexdump -C does, on a black background like a terminal's. It is selected a byte at a time. The byte's two hex digits are marked in the primary selection colour, and the character that stands for it in the right-hand column in the secondary one, so that a reader can find the same byte in both. A byte that is not printable shows as ..

This node also has a name, and its content asked for a row for a toolbar with DataNodeContent::with_toolbar. So Scene::show_selection_toolbar draws the bar inside the node's own box, under its name, instead of at the edge of the canvas.
