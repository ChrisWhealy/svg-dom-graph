Weighted springs

In this weighted graph, the edges behave as springs whose stiffness corresponds to the edge weight: a spring with the weight 10 is ten times as hard to stretch as one with the weight 1.

Drag any circle and the other circles will be pulled and pushed.

Let go and the network will settles under damping, and stop moving once it is at rest.

Weights represent spring stiffness, not length. Reset puts the circles back, gives the springs new random weights and a random tension, and so sets the network moving again.

The circle you hold follows the pointer straight away, but as far as the springs are concerned, they see a proxy node that moves at a fixed top speed, so a sudden jump reaches its neighbours via a sequence of small, connected moves, not as one enormous stretch.

During a very fast drag the held circle can therefore pass over a neighbour for a moment before the neighbour moves out of the way.

Sleeping: the graph is at rest.
