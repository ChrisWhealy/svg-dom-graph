Amplitude, Frequency and Phase

A single sine wave is described by just three properties:

Property Unit Description
Amplitude Depends on what is being measured.
For example, signal strength in decibels		 How high is the wave
Frequency Hz Cycles per second
Phase φ (The Greek letter Phi) How far through its cycle is the wave at some specific point in time (usually t = 0)

These values can be combined in the formula y = A · sin(2π · f · t + φ).

Use the three sliders in the diagram, or Tab to one and use the arrow keys, to change the wave.

The time axis always shows one second, so a higher frequency shows that more cycles occur in the same one second time interval.
