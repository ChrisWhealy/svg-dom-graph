Ellipses and circles

An ellipse is simply a circle with two differing radii. If you want to create a circle, then you effectively create an ellipse with only one radius.

When you add a label to an ellipse, the label keeps its normal size and the ellipse will stretch to accommodate it by the same factor in both directions. This means that the ellispe or circle will always maintain its aspect ratio,
