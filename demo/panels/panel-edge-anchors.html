Fixing points

The parent starts with one child. The straight connector between them attaches at each node's centre, but stops at whichever boundary point that ray crosses first.

Move the slider to 1 and the connector snaps onto the exact midpoint of the side it crosses. Move it higher and more children appear, each sharing the same parent. The connectors then settle on their own evenly spaced fixing point along the parent's side. Move it back down and children disappear again.
